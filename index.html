How it works.

Three steps. No setup, no dashboard.

01

Meeting starts

Levi watches for microphone activity. The moment a meeting app activates the mic, recording begins automatically — no clicks, no timers.

02

Meeting ends

When mic activity stops, Levi stops recording. The audio is mixed, optionally denoised, and saved as a compact .m4a — all in the background.

03

Get audio + transcript

A .m4a and a .txt transcript with speaker labels land in your chosen folder. Ready to search, share, or archive.

What Levi is
"Levi is not a meeting bot and not a cloud notes service. It's a quiet macOS app that captures mic and system audio, optionally cleans the recording, and creates a local transcript you can actually use."
