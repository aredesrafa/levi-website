How it works.

Three steps. No setup, no dashboard.

01

Click to record

Open Levi from your menu bar and start recording. Or enable Auto Mode and let Levi begin the moment your meeting app activates the mic.

02

Meeting ends

Stop manually, or let Levi detect when your meeting ends. The audio is mixed, optionally denoised, and saved as a compact .m4a.

03

Get audio + transcript

A .m4a and a .txt transcript with speaker labels land in your chosen folder. Ready to search, share, or archive.
